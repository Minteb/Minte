Taking precision agriculture to the sky

August to September 2026. Our first multispectral UAV campaign for crop mapping and yield estimation, flown over wheat in West Shewa, East Shewa and West Arsi zones as part of the ADD-IN Ethiopia project (CGIAR SPIA).

From LinkedIn, September 2026

eBee X over agricultural fields

Today we launched a field mapping campaign using the eBee X fixed-wing UAV equipped with a multispectral sensor payload.

Fixed-wing UAVs offer unmatched efficiency for broad-acre field surveys, letting us capture high-throughput multispectral imagery across large agricultural landscapes. By capturing distinct spectral bands, including near-infrared (NIR) and red edge, we can compute vegetation indices like NDVI and NDRE to detect early plant stress, evaluate canopy structure and monitor crop health before symptoms become visible to the naked eye.

Earth observation for the fields that feed Ethiopia

I am a PhD candidate in Geospatial Science at the School of Earth Sciences, Addis Ababa University. My research asks how to map crops and estimate yields reliably on small, mixed, cloud-covered farms, where standard satellite methods often fail.

My dissertation develops probabilistic crop mapping and adaptive stratification for smallholder yield estimation across Ethiopian agro-ecological systems, combining Sentinel imagery, UAV surveys, crop-cut data and machine learning. Before the PhD I spent eight years lecturing at Mattu University, including a term as academic dean, and taught at Wollega University.

Alongside research I consult on geospatial analytics for agricultural programmes, and I founded SpatialWise, a training platform building skills in remote sensing, GIS and GeoAI across East Africa.
